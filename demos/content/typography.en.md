The site has `smart_punctuation = false`; this page turns it on in its front
matter.

"Double quotes" and 'single quotes' curl. Two hyphens -- an en dash, three
--- an em dash. Three dots... become an ellipsis.

Code is left alone: `"straight" -- ...`.
